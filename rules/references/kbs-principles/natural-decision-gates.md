# Natural Decision Gates

> Create explicit checkpoints between phases where stakeholders align on direction, gather input, and decide whether to proceed.

---

## Core Idea

Don't let decisions happen by default or drift. Make decision moments visible and intentional.

---

## Why It Matters

- **Stakeholder alignment** — gate moments force explicit communication and buy-in
- **Clear ownership** — visible gates make clear who decides and when
- **Course correction** — gates allow pausing to reassess if direction is sound
- **Resource efficiency** — gates prevent investing heavily in wrong direction
- **Psychological safety** — structured gates prevent "surprise" rejections late in the process

---

## How to Apply

### Structure
- Define phases with clear entry/exit criteria
- Create checkpoints between phases
- Specify who makes the decision at each gate
- Document decision rationale

### At Each Gate
- Present findings/prototypes/artifacts
- Gather diverse input
- Make explicit go/no-go decision
- De-prioritize what's *not* being pursued (prevents distraction)
- Communicate decision and reasoning to broader team

### Gate Examples
- Discovery → Define: "Do we agree on the problem?"
- Define → Develop: "Are we solving the right problem?"
- Develop → Deliver: "Which solutions are we committing to?"
- Post-Deliver: "Did it work? What's next?"
