# Solve the Right Problem First

> Rather than rushing to solve the immediately obvious problem, invest time in exploration and discovery to ensure your efforts are targeted at the root cause.

---

## Core Idea

Don't skip discovery. Understand the problem deeply before building solutions.

**Anti-pattern:** Rushing to "find a new office" when the real question is "how do we best support deep work and collaboration?"

---

## Why It Matters

- **Misaligned effort** — solving the wrong problem wastes resources and disappoints users
- **Root causes** — surface problems often mask deeper systemic issues
- **Solution fitness** — solutions designed without discovery are brittle and miss user needs

---

## How to Apply

1. **Zoom out** — expand the problem statement before narrowing
2. **Ask "why" multiple times** — trace problems to their causes (Five Whys technique)
3. **Involve affected users** — understand their perspective, not your assumptions
4. **Document the problem space** — map scope, complexity, underlying context
5. **Tie solutions back to evidence** — ensure you're solving what you discovered, not what you assumed
