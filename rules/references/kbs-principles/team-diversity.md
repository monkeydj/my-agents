# Team Diversity

> Draw on different strengths, perspectives, and thinking styles. Involve people from different disciplines, backgrounds, and expertise.

---

## Core Idea

Diverse teams generate better ideas, catch blind spots, and create more robust solutions.

---

## Why It Matters

- **Blind spots** — homogeneous teams miss what different perspectives reveal
- **Richer ideas** — diverse experience pools generate more creative solutions
- **Better decisions** — diverse debate surfaces tradeoffs and risks that consensus-seeking misses
- **Psychological safety** — teams with diversity of thought feel safer to contribute

---

## How to Apply

### In Divergent Phases
- Invite free-thinking spirits, analytical minds, practical doers
- Protect early-stage ideas from premature critique
- Seek input from people outside the immediate problem domain
- Cross-functional brainstorming

### In Convergent Phases
- Analytical and practical types excel at scrutiny and prioritization
- Diverse perspectives on priorities prevent groupthink
- Different expertise surfaces implementation risks early
- Debate different solutions before committing

### Ongoing
- Rotate team composition across projects
- Explicitly seek perspectives from underrepresented disciplines
- Document reasoning, not just decisions, so diversity of thought is captured
