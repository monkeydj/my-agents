# Diverge Before Converge

> Expand options and explore breadth before narrowing focus and making commitments.

---

## Core Idea

Separate thinking modes. Diverge *first* (generate, explore, question), then converge (decide, prioritize, commit).

**Anti-pattern:** Brainstorming with gatekeepers in the room. Shooting down ideas before exploring their potential. Premature convergence kills creativity.

---

## Why It Matters

- **Kills fragile ideas** — early critique shuts down exploration before ideas mature
- **Analysis paralysis** — converging too slowly on analysis without clear decision points
- **Missed alternatives** — narrowing too fast locks in the first adequate solution, not the best
- **Team morale** — clear separation signals creative thinking is safe and valued

---

## How to Apply

### Divergent Thinking
- Generate options without judgment
- Quantity over quality
- Encourage wild, out-of-the-box thinking
- Document everything
- Involve diverse perspectives

### Convergent Thinking
- Synthesize and categorize findings
- Make tough choices with evidence
- Align team on priorities
- Create natural decision gates
- Communicate what's *not* being pursued
